about me

My name is Isaac, I was born and raised in Brasil and at the age of 9, moved to Massachusetts. I attended a vocational high school and specialized in veterinary science, during which I worked at a vet clinic for around 1.5 years and volunteered with animal shelter surgeries. My academic interests now span neurodegenerative disorders, memory formation and loss, and consciousness. I am in my second year at Yale, and I study molecular biology. I hope to pursue a career in research, likely in translational work (but that is for future me to decide). I've previously interned at the Nehme Lab (Broad Institute), Kwon Lab (Ragon Institute), and now conduct Alzheimer's research in the Datta Lab (Yale Med School). Other academic interests include religious studies, languages, and current politics.
